# Literature search strategy

Prepared on 2 November 2026 from the local survey manuscripts and the author's clarification. This document consolidates the documented strategy; it is not an original search-execution log or a new search report.

## Search period

The author recalls conducting the searches from early June to approximately 20 June 2026. The manuscripts describe coverage as extending through June 2026. Exact source-specific execution dates have not been recovered.

- arXiv
- ACL Anthology
- OpenReview
- Major computer vision and machine-learning conference proceedings
- Major natural-language-processing conference proceedings
- Google Scholar
- Backward and forward citation chasing from seed papers

## Representative query templates

These four templates are preserved in the local manuscript version `main_v4.tex`, in its Search sources and strategy subsection. They are representative expressions, not a verified record of the syntax submitted to every individual platform.

### Multimodal uncertainty, confidence and calibration

```text
("multimodal large language model" OR "large vision-language model" OR "vision-language model")
AND (uncertainty OR confidence OR calibration)
```

### Selective prediction, abstention and answerability

```text
("visual question answering" OR VQA OR "video question answering" OR OCR OR chart)
AND (selective prediction OR abstention OR answerability OR unanswerable)
```

### Conformal prediction and risk control

```text
("multimodal" OR "vision-language")
AND (conformal prediction OR risk control)
```

### Grounding, hallucination detection and knowledge boundaries

```text
("MLLM" OR "LVLM")
AND (grounding OR hallucination detection OR knowledge boundary OR IDK)
```
